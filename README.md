# Jewellery-Retail-Sales-Analytics


## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **Jewellery Retail Analytics** solution designed to analyze sales performance, store operations, inventory movement, customer behavior, and product-level performance.

The project uses **Excel, SQL, and Power BI** to transform 100K+ retail records into actionable business insights that can support decisions related to inventory management, store performance, sales growth, and customer targeting.

The primary objective was to answer:

> **Which stores, products, and customer segments are driving sales, where are inventory inefficiencies occurring, and what actions can improve overall retail performance?**

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on the following business questions:

- Which stores are performing the best in terms of sales?
- Which jewellery categories and products generate the highest revenue?
- How does sales performance vary across stores?
- Which products contribute the most to overall sales?
- Where are inventory levels high or low?
- Which products may require inventory optimization?
- What customer segments contribute the most to revenue?
- What are the key sales and retail KPIs?
- Are there inconsistencies or quality issues in the underlying data?
- What business actions can improve sales and inventory efficiency?

# 📊 Dataset

The project uses a retail jewellery dataset containing **100K+ records** covering multiple aspects of the retail business.

The dataset includes information related to:

- Stores
- Jewellery products
- Sales transactions
- Customers
- Inventory
- Product/category information
- Revenue and sales metrics

The data was analyzed at transaction, product, store, and customer levels depending on the business question.
